# Fallout 4 World Knowledge Attribution

The starter catalog in `fallout_worldknowledge_parity_v1.csv` is derived from
the reviewed Fallout 4 summaries previously shipped in
`fallout_worldknowledge_basic.csv`. Source URLs and revision metadata are
recorded in `fallout_worldknowledge_sources.jsonl`.

Those summaries are based on articles from [The Fallout
Wiki](https://fallout.wiki/), whose article content is generally available
under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license
unless an article states otherwise. The derived summaries are distributed
under the same license.
