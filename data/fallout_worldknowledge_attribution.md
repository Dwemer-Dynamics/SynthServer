# Fallout 4 World Knowledge Attribution

The 125-topic catalog in `fallout_worldknowledge_parity_v1.csv` combines 15
corrected starter summaries with 40 source-bound Commonwealth summaries and 50 additional public regional/occupation summaries.
The everyday lore pass adds 20 sourced topics and revises the existing farming
summary; the other 104 existing articles retain their authored text.

## Inherited starter attribution

The original starter summaries were previously shipped in
`fallout_worldknowledge_basic.csv`. The inherited notice credits
[The Fallout Wiki](https://fallout.wiki/) and its contributors under
CC BY-SA 4.0. That credit remains applicable to the inherited material.
The old JSONL instead recorded a generic Bethesda landing page and a dated
revision label. Those records are preserved under `legacy_source_record` for
the 15 corrected topics; they are historical receipts, not verified immutable
page revisions. No missing revision identifier has been invented.

## New source summaries and corrections

The new articles and source-checked starter corrections adapt the named
[Fallout Wiki at Fandom](https://fallout.fandom.com/) articles by their
contributors. Their original wiki text is credited under
[CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/),
consistent with [Fandom's licensing policy](https://www.fandom.com/licensing).
Article, page-history and license links accompany each topic in
`fallout_worldknowledge_sources.jsonl`. Original source license records remain
3.0; the concise adaptations use 4.0 as permitted by section 4(b) of the
[3.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode).
The Lexington summary also lists a short factual corroboration from Gamer
Guides, separately credited in its source URLs; that page is not labeled as
Creative Commons licensed.

Changes made on 7 October 2026: concise paraphrases, independent basic and
advanced knowledge tiers, neutral pre-war product descriptions, restrictions
on personal/faction knowledge, and omission of quest outcomes and hidden
identities. Source records identify the reviewed sections. General catalogs
do not establish current nearby actors, weather, player appearance or learned
relationships. No verbatim game dialogue or game images, voice recordings or
other game assets are included. Revision identifiers are null when the browser
returned no reliable immutable revision metadata.

Local editorial approval means this preparation passed the local content
review. It does not imply approval by Rangroo, Bethesda or a beta release.

## Selected DLC and occupation expansion

The 8 October expansion adds 50 public topics across Far Harbor, Nuka-World, robot technology, workshop technology and Commonwealth livelihoods. The sources and their contributors retain CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution; these concise adaptations join the catalog under CC BY-SA 4.0. Sources were read through indexed article extracts; no immutable revision or raw-page snapshot is claimed. Detailed regional expertise requires the scoped classes documented in the vocabulary. Hidden identities, private experiments and future quest outcomes are omitted. This selected catalog is incomplete.

## Everyday lore accuracy

The later 8 October pass adds 20 concise topics for crops, food, caps, chems and
American consumer culture. In-game dialogue/loading-screen/item evidence is
identified through the cited article/transcript pages. The farming correction
distinguishes tatos from ordinary tomatoes; no blanket extinction claim or
disputed Jet-origin account is asserted. Addictol's uncertain prewar dating stays
in source review notes and does not grant prewar access. Brand recognition does
not manufacture inventory, and basic knowledge does not grant specialist facts.
The source records retain uncertainty, attribution and null unverified revision
IDs. No proprietary audio/assets or verbatim dialogue are distributed.
