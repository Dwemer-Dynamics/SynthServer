# Fallout 4 named characters and common object descriptions

The selected base-game and DLC content pack contains concise adaptations of the Fallout Wiki
articles identified in `fallout4_content_sources.json`, credited to their respective
contributors. Each row retains its source link; Fandom sources include the page
history link, access date and original CC BY-SA 3.0 license. These adaptations are
distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/),
preserving attribution and share-alike separately from the application's code license.
The original text's license is documented by [Fandom's licensing policy](https://www.fandom.com/licensing).

Changes: concise paraphrase, positive self-identity, conservative appearance and
editorial character style, explicit knowledge access tags, exact actor/object
bindings, and omission of hidden identities or unobserved quest outcomes. No article, image, audio, master file, save or proprietary asset is redistributed.
One short canonical Takahashi order phrase is retained as a speech constraint.

Independent Fallout Wiki access returned HTTP 403 during preparation. Available
search-indexed Fallout Wiki/Fandom articles were used instead. Page revision IDs
were not returned; the receipt uses an access date and history link, not a fabricated
revision. Brief factual corroboration from Game8/GameItems is identified separately
per object; no source prose from those sites is copied. Some visual descriptions
remain broad because finer detail was not established.

Form IDs, record types and editor identities were checked read-only against the
locally owned game master. The private extraction table is not shipped; only the
authored catalog's verified identifiers are included. Static facts never establish
current equipment, companionship, familiarity or player accomplishments.

Fallout and related names are trademarks of their respective owners. This content
is not endorsed by Bethesda Softworks. Local installation does not establish public
release acceptance; paired gameplay and the repository's publication gates remain open.

The current catalog contains 204 templates. The 8 October completion researched
80 additional actor rows and imported 76 suitable live identities plus four
Deacon public covers. Four authored identities are withheld with explicit reasons
in `fallout4_character_completion_review.json`. Curie, Virgil and Deacon received
conservative continuity guidance; the other original templates and all 83 object
descriptions are preserved. Three physical aliases share existing biographies.
The short fixed Takahashi order phrase is the only quoted dialogue added.

Additional sources include the linked official Prima guide for factual
corroboration and alternate community-wiki pages when direct access failed. Their
source links and access method are recorded individually; no page text was copied
wholesale. Character delivery and goals are restrained authored interpretations.
No proprietary master files, scripts, audio or raw extraction tables are shipped.
